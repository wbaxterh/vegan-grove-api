# SOUL.md: what Vegan Grove is

Vegan Grove is a privacy-first activism engine for Southern California's vegan community. Every member is an activist. The app helps them find sanctuaries, vegan businesses, events, and each other, and then get out the door and act.

## Three forces

- **Compassion.** Everything here exists for the animals. Features that do not eventually help an animal are decoration.
- **Action.** A sanctuary volunteer day, a vigil, a cube of truth, a potluck that turns a curious friend vegan. The app is measured by actions taken in the real world, not by time spent in it.
- **Community.** Activism is lonely without a crew. The app makes it easy to find your people and hard for anyone else to find them.

## The loop

**Learn, Plan, Act, Share, Improve.** Learn from Guides and the media library. Plan with Events and Places. Act in the world. Share with friends and your Grove. Improve by looking at your own action log and picking the next thing.

## Ethos

- **Activists first.** Organizers and members are the customers. Businesses and orgs are guests.
- **Privacy is the floor, not a feature.** Collect the minimum. Profiles are never public. Default to friends-only. If a feature needs more data than it deserves, the feature changes, not the data policy.
- **Open about the system, closed about the people.** The code, the docs, the roadmap, and the data inventory are public. Who is a member, who went where, and who talks to whom are not.
- **Utility with soul.** Cyberpunk on the surface: neon on near-black, monospace accents, terminal honesty. Underneath: fast, correct, and boring in the best way.
- **Simplicity is discipline.** One way to do each thing. Fewer screens, sharper ones.
- **Culture over clout.** No follower counts, no public leaderboards, no engagement bait. Love reactions, no dunking.

## Principles

- Action over vanity. A feature that produces one more sanctuary volunteer beats one that produces a thousand scrolls.
- Practical over technical. The chain, the model, the encryption are plumbing. Members see outcomes.
- Focus over flood. Ship one improvement, measure it, then the next.
- Cohesion over widgets. Every screen follows Context, Action, Support: what is this, what can I do, what helps me do it.

## The companion

Ivy (working name) is a companion, not a mascot. Ivy answers questions about places, events, and going vegan, drafts outreach messages, and helps plan a first sanctuary visit. Ivy never sees a member's email, area, friends, or messages, and forgets conversations that are not pinned.

## Feedback operating system

Ship one improvement. Capture feedback in the app, anonymized and aggregated. Measure one signal per change. Review weekly. Rubric: Adoption (did people use it), Clarity (did they understand it), Impact (did an action happen). If two of three fail after one iteration, simplify or sunset.

## Build order

Trust, then the core loop, then delight, then expansion. Trust means auth, privacy defaults, deletion, and encryption are right before anything else ships.

## Shipping questions

1. What activist outcome does this improve?
2. What data does it touch, and is that the minimum?
3. What breaks if it is wrong, and how do we roll back?
4. What one signal tells us it worked?

## Compass

Vegan Grove should feel like the sharpest, kindest organizer you know: knows every sanctuary, every vegan spot, every action this month, and would never give out your number.
